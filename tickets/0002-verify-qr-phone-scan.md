# 0002. Verify a phone scans the addon QR

* **Status:** open
* **Kind:** verification
* **Priority:** P1
* **Source:** docs/spike-s0.md; ADR 0014
* **ADRs:** none
* **EGM:** [initial draft](../docs/egm/0002-verify-qr-phone-scan/design_draft.md) (2026-11-22, unreviewed)

## What

Scan the addon QR drawn with `SetColorTexture` with two phone scanners at UI scales 0.8, 1.0 and 1.2.

## Done when

Result recorded in `docs/spike-s0.md`; the QR is changed if it fails.
